FAQ

Is my file private?

Yes. Your file never leaves your computer and is never saved anywhere. It disappears when you click Start over or close this page.

Why is my download a .zip?

Browsers can't save a .shp file on its own here, so it comes inside a .zip. Double-click the .zip to open it and you'll find your .shp files inside.

Why are there up to three .shp files?

A shapefile can hold only one kind of shape, so places (points), paths (lines) and areas (polygons) each get their own file.

How do I open the shapefile?

In QGIS, drag the .shp file onto the map. In ArcGIS Pro, use Map → Add Data and pick the .shp. ArcGIS also needs the extra shapefile parts, and so does seeing names and descriptions in any program: in step 3, turn on "Include the extra shapefile parts", then download again.

My file is over 1 MB. What can I do?

Save it as a .kmz instead of a .kml. A .kmz is compressed and is often much smaller. In Google Earth, right-click the folder, choose Save Place As, and pick .kmz. If it's still too big, save each folder as its own file and add them here together.
